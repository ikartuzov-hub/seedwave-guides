Digitalising a café or restaurant in Madeira in 2026: is any funding open, and can pre-order be subsidised?

Short, honest answer: there is currently no open call in Madeira dedicated solely to SME digitalisation. The two regional calls that funded a technology component in 2025–2026 have both closed. What you can do now is have the application ready before the next window opens — because they last weeks, not months.

Quick answer

If you are looking for a grant to install pre-ordering, online payment, POS or management software in your café in Madeira: right now there is no open regional line for it. There was one, and there will be again — but what separates those who get funded from those who don't is not the idea, it is having the paperwork ready on the day the call is published.

What Madeira had — and when it closed

Closed 30.09.2025

Inovação 2030 — productive innovation (M2030-2025-26)

Published 5 August 2025, applications from 5 August to 30 September 2025, 17:00. Budget €10 million ERDF. Base rate 25%, rising up to 45% depending on company size, creation of qualified jobs and location (IDR-RAM).

Actual window: about 8 weeks.

Closed 15.05.2026

Internacionalização 2030 — includes e-commerce (M2030-2026-13)

Published 1 April 2026, applications from 1 April to 15 May 2026, 17:00. Budget €1 million ERDF, co-funding up to 40%. Supported actions included foreign-market prospecting, international marketing, e-commerce, brand creation and promotion, certification (Madeira 2030).

Actual window: about 6 weeks.

Open until 10.09.2026 — but it is energy

SIEED — Energy Efficiency 2030 (M2030-2026-21)

It is the only business call currently open in Madeira that is relevant to hospitality, but it does not cover digitalisation: it funds refrigeration, HVAC, lighting and low-consumption equipment. If your investment also has an energy component, see the SIEED guide — that deadline is firm.

Golden rule: do not buy the software or sign the implementation contract before submitting an application. Spending incurred before submission stops being eligible. Ask suppliers for proforma invoices — never actual invoices — and keep them.

What to prepare now, with no call open

Four things, all free or nearly free, and all slower than they look:

  1. SME certification — free online application via IAPMEI. It is an access condition in most incentive schemes.
  2. Clean standing with the tax authority and social security. One small forgotten debt sinks the application at the final step.
  3. Supplier proformas — software, licences, implementation, staff training, itemised. Not invoices.
  4. Project description — what you digitalise, why, and the expected outcome (service time, order errors, prepaid revenue). It is what the assessor reads first.

With that in the drawer, submitting stops being a month of work and becomes a few days. That is exactly the difference between making and missing a six-week window.

FAQ

Is there any open Madeira grant to digitalise my café?

As of 24 July 2026, there is no open call dedicated solely to SME digitalisation in the Region. Inovação 2030 closed on 30.09.2025 and Internacionalização 2030 on 15.05.2026. Always check madeira.portugal2030.pt, as this changes with each published call.

Is pre-order software an eligible cost?

In calls that have funded digitalisation, software, licences and implementation services typically qualify as eligible costs within an approved investment project. It is not automatic — it depends on the call in force and how the project is framed. With no call open, nothing is eligible today.

If nothing is open, why prepare now?

Because the windows are short: Inovação 2030 was open for about 8 weeks, Internacionalização 2030 for about 6. Anyone who starts gathering documents only after the announcement rarely submits in time.

What about national digitalisation vouchers?

National instruments supporting SME digital transition do exist, with their own rules and coverage — not all of them include Madeira, and their windows open and close. We do not publish percentages or amounts here that are not confirmed in the text of a call in force. When there is a concrete applicable call, this guide is updated with its number and dates.

Does SIEED cover digitalisation?

No. SIEED (M2030-2026-21) is energy efficiency and decarbonisation. POS, pre-ordering and management software are not covered by that call. There is one exception worth knowing: software, licences and sensors for energy monitoring and management are an eligible cost under SIEED. Software does fit SIEED — the energy kind, not the commercial kind. See the SIEED guide.
